## Project Overview
This analysis explores a asynthetic financial dataset for **GearTickler Inc** to uncover trends in overall sales, product and country distribution, and forcast future revenue. The goal of this analysis is to unlock actionable insights to improve overall sales and profitability.
## Data Source
This data set is provided by Microsoft. You can download it here: [Financial Sample Download](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/sample-financial-download).

## Insights and Projections
### Sales Insights
- Highest grossing product is Passeo with a total of  $33,011,143.95 amounting to 27.8% of total sales.
- Governments were the biggest clients with a total sales of $52,504,260.67 (44% of total) with government purchases dominating the top 10% of transactions.
- The United States of America stands out as the biggest market with  $25,029,830.17 in total sales.
- France remains the most profitable market with  $3,781,020.78 net profit surpasing US and Canada.
- Despite being the lowest grossing product, **Carretera** remains the **most profitable** product available with a 30% profit margin.
### Next Quarter Projection
Using a 3-month Simple Moving Average chart, the average sales for the next quarter, Q1 2015 is projected at $9,590,670.
## Recommendations
- Invest more in the France Market to increase the overall profitability of the company.
- Invest in boosting sales across the Small Business and Enterprise segments to reduce sales risk by lowering Government sales contribution to <30%.
- Consider spending on more Marketing in Mexico to boost sales.
- Consider spending on more marketing for Carretera to boost sales and increase overall profit margin.
## Conclusion
Through this finalcial analysis, we are able to uncover trends in sales market demographics, product popularity and segment strength. These insights can inform stakeolders particularly Sales and Marketing Execs on how to optimize business development and advertising allocate financial resources and increase the bottom line.
